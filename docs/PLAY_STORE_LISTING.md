## 3. Full Description

**GuruStudy** is the complete, offline-first study companion and smart syllabus management application built exclusively for Class 10th NCERT & CBSE students for the **2026–2027 academic session**.

Prepare with confidence for your 2027 Board Exams with our interactive **93-Day Preparation Roadmap (15 November 2026 – 15 February 2027)**, complete official NCERT chapter summaries, exam-oriented practice questions, formula master sheets, and personalized voice study alarms that call you by name!

### 🌟 Key Features of GuruStudy:

#### 1. 📅 Interactive 93-Day Exam Roadmap (15 Nov 2026 – 15 Feb 2027)
- Pre-loaded, scientifically balanced day-by-day syllabus schedule.
- Covers Mathematics, Science, Social Science, English, Hindi, and IT.
- Easily customize any date: tap to adjust study duration, change topics, reschedule missed sessions, or mark chapters complete.

#### 2. ⏰ Smart Personalized Voice Study Alarms
- Never miss a study session! GuruStudy integrates with Android's exact alarm clock APIs (`AlarmManager`).
- Rings with a calming study chime and announces your name and scheduled chapter via Text-to-Speech (TTS):
  *"Hello Rahul, it’s time to study Electricity on GuruStudy!"*
  *(हिंदी: "राहुल जी, अब आपका विज्ञान का पाठ विद्युत पढ़ने का समय हो गया है!")*
- Includes full-screen alarm display, 10-minute snooze, and instant study timer launcher.

- **Mathematics:** Real Numbers, Polynomials, Linear Equations, Quadratics, AP, Triangles, Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, Circles, Areas, Surface Areas & Volumes, Statistics, Probability.

- **Social Science:** History, Geography, Democratic Politics, and Economics.
- **English:** First Flight (Prose & Poems) and Footprints Without Feet.

- Active countdown timer (15, 30, 45, 60, 90 mins).
