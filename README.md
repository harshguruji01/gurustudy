## 📅 The 5 Roadmap Phases (Nov 15, 2026 – Feb 15, 2027)

1. **Phase 1: Foundation Building (16 Days • Nov 15 – Nov 30)**
   - High-yield fundamentals: Real Numbers, Statistics, Chemical Reactions, Life Processes, Forest & Wildlife, Water Resources.
2. **Phase 2: Core Consolidation (15 Days • Dec 01 – Dec 15)**
   - Arithmetic Progressions, Triangles, Coordinate Geometry, Power Sharing, Federalism, Nationalism in India.
3. **Phase 3: Advanced Concepts & Application (16 Days • Dec 16 – Dec 31)**
   - Trigonometric Applications, Circles, Surface Areas, Metals & Non-metals, Heredity, Light & Human Eye.
4. **Phase 4: Full Revision & Weak Spot Remediation (15 Days • Jan 01 – Jan 15)**
   - Probability, Carbon Compounds, Civics synthesis, Economics Globalization, Map practice.
5. **Phase 5: Final Board Sprint (31 Days • Jan 16 – Feb 15)**
   - Full 3-hour sample papers, CBSE marking scheme alignment, time management, and mock board tests.
