Frequently Asked Questions

Does GuruStudy work without an internet connection? +
Yes! Both the Android APK and the Web App (PWA) are built with offline-first persistence. The timetable, voice alarms, formula vaults, and chapter checkboxes work 100% offline. Internet is only needed to stream YouTube lectures.
What if I cannot start at 4:00 PM every day? +
In the Profile / Settings tab, you can customize your Daily Start Time to any hour (e.g. 5:00 PM, 6:00 PM). All 4 study blocks and 10-minute breaks will auto-adjust sequentially.
Is the Artificial Intelligence (Subject Code 417) syllabus included? +
Yes. The complete CBSE AI 417 syllabus (both Part A Employability Skills and Part B Subject Specific Skills across 13 units) is distributed over the 13 Sundays between 20 Nov 2026 and 15 Feb 2027.
How does the 88-Day Streak calculation work? +
The streak is strictly bound to 20 November 2026 – 15 February 2027 (88 Days). Before 20 Nov, the app operates in Warm-up Mode with streak locked at 0d. On 20 Nov, Day 1 begins.
