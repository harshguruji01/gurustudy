The Master 4-Hour Daily Revision Routine

Strictly aligned with the Board Exam Revision Study Plan PDF. Each subject is placed in its optimal cognitive energy slot to maximize retention and eliminate burnout.

🌅 06:00 AM – 07:30 AM

Mathematics

⏱️ 1.5 Hours (90 Mins) Daily

Cognitive Strategy: Peak morning mental energy is dedicated to high-friction numerical problem solving, algebraic manipulation, and theorem proofs before school fatigue sets in.

Core Chapters: Real Numbers, Polynomials, Linear Equations, Quadratic Equations, Arithmetic Progressions, Triangles, Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, Circles, Statistics.
🔬 05:30 PM – 06:30 PM

Science

⏱️ 1.0 Hour (60 Mins) Daily

Cognitive Strategy: Post-school afternoon revival session focusing on conceptual understanding, chemical reactions, balancing, biological diagrams, and physics ray numericals.

Core Chapters: Chemical Reactions, Acids Bases & Salts, Metals & Non-metals, Carbon Compounds, Life Processes, Control & Coordination, Reproduction, Heredity, Light, Electricity.
🌍 07:00 PM – 08:00 PM

Social Science

⏱️ 1.0 Hour (60 Mins) Daily

Cognitive Strategy: Active recall, chronology construction, cause-and-effect flowcharts, and map pointing across History, Geography, Political Science, and Economics.

Core Chapters: Rise of Nationalism in Europe, Nationalism in India, Resources & Development, Water Resources, Agriculture, Power Sharing, Federalism, Money & Credit.
📖 08:30 PM – 09:00 PM

English Language

⏱️ 0.5 Hour (30 Mins) Daily

Cognitive Strategy: Low-stress pre-dinner wind-down focusing on chapter comprehension, character traits, grammar error-spotting, and analytical paragraph structure.

Core Books: First Flight (Prose & Poetry), Footprints Without Feet, Formal Letter Writing, Analytical Paragraphs, and Integrated Grammar exercises.

The Sunday Backlog Buffer Rule (Golden Rule)

As highlighted in the master PDF, consistency beats intensity. Every Sunday features a dedicated 4.0-hour catch-up block. Students must use this window to clear any incomplete daily targets from Monday through Saturday before starting the next week. If all targets are met, Sunday is used for weak spot drills and formula sheet consolidation.
